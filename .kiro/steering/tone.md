# Tone

**Spooky but kind.** The crypt is atmospheric; the guide is on the learner's side.

## Do
- Short, plain sentences. Spooky flavour in nouns and verbs, never at the cost of clarity.
  - Good: "A ghost rises. You'll meet this one again before the final door."
  - Good: "The candle catches. One step closer to the light."
- Encourage after mistakes: "Not this time. Here's what your notes say."
- Make errors helpful: say what happened and what to do next.
  - "This page looks like a scan, so we can't read its text. Remove it or paste the text instead."
- Use "your notes", not "the source document".

## Don't
- Mock, scold or shame the learner, ever.
- Use horror that frightens: no gore, threats or jump-scare language.
- Overclaim accuracy or learning results (see `evidence-and-honesty.md`).
- Use jargon in UI copy: say "question", not "item"; "challenge", not "dispute ticket".
